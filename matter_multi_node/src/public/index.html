How It Works

Devices are not announced by default to prevent network congestion. Click "Announce as Matter Device" on any switch, light, climate unit, or sensor you want in Google Home. It allocates a dedicated UDP port (starting at 5541) and generates its QR code.

  1. Click "Announce as Matter Device" on your desired device below.
  2. Open Google Home on your phone > Devices > + (Add) > Matter-enabled device.
  3. Scan the QR code (or enter the manual pairing code). Once paired, Google Home connects directly via local Matter!
