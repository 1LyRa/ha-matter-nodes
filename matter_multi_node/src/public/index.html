How to Pair with Google Home

  1. Open the Google Home app on your phone.
  2. Tap Devices > + (Add) > Matter-enabled device.
  3. Scan the QR Code of the switch below directly from your screen, or choose "Set up without code" and enter the Manual Pairing Code.
  4. Repeat for any switch you need in Google Home. Once paired, Google Home controls it directly over local Matter!
